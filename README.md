AI Plant Care Assistant

My idea is to develop an AI-powered application called "Smart Plant Care". This application helps people take better care of their plants using artificial intelligence.

Users can take a picture of their plants and upload it to the application. The AI analyzes the image to identify the plant species and detect possible problems, such as diseases, dry leaves, or lack of water.

The application can also provide personalized recommendations about watering, sunlight, temperature, and soil conditions. For example, if a plant needs water, the AI sends a notification to the user.

Another useful feature is a smart watering schedule that adjusts automatically based on weather conditions and the plant's needs.

The main goal of this project is to make plant care easier, save water, prevent plant diseases, and help people keep their plants healthy.

This application can be useful for beginners, gardeners, and anyone who loves plants.# AI-Plant-Care-Assistant
